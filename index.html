Beginner Guide

How to Buy RECEH Token

The RECEH sale page is a Web3 DApp. For the best mobile experience, open this page directly through the DApp browser inside your wallet application.

1

Open your wallet's DApp Browser

Use a compatible Web3 wallet such as MetaMask, TokenPocket, Bitget Wallet, Trust Wallet, or another wallet that supports DApp browsing.

2

Open the official RECEH purchase page

Copy the URL below, then paste it into the DApp browser of your wallet.

https://cryptoreceh.com/buy/
3

Connect your wallet

When the page is open, tap Connect Wallet and approve the wallet connection request.

4

Use BNB Smart Chain (BSC)

Make sure your wallet is connected to BNB Smart Chain (BSC) before making the purchase.

5

Enter the amount of BNB

Enter how much BNB you want to spend. The page will automatically show the estimated amount of RECEH you will receive.

6

Confirm the purchase

Tap Buy RECEH, review the transaction in your wallet, and confirm it. Wait until the transaction is successfully processed on BNB Smart Chain.

7

Add RECEH to your wallet — optional

After a successful purchase, tap Add RECEH to Wallet if you want RECEH to appear in your wallet's token list.

Important: Only confirm transactions that you understand. Make sure you are using the official RECEH purchase page and the BNB Smart Chain network before sending BNB.

Help Center

Buy RECEH with BNB FAQ

Quick answers about the network, purchase process, token contract, and transaction verification.

What network is used to buy RECEH?

RECEH purchases on this page use BNB Smart Chain (BSC), chain ID 56. Your wallet must be connected to BNB Smart Chain before confirming a purchase.

What do I need to buy RECEH?

You need a compatible Web3 wallet with enough BNB for the purchase and network transaction fee. The minimum purchase amount shown on this page is read from the sale contract and can change.

How is the RECEH amount calculated?

The estimated RECEH amount is calculated from the sale contract's current on-chain rate. The final amount is determined by the smart contract when the transaction is executed.

What is the RECEH token contract?

The RECEH token contract on BNB Smart Chain is 0x4c9C431Fa7fD104c0E7230d20E1623E62019A1C5. Always verify the contract address on BscScan before interacting with a token.

Where can I verify a RECEH transaction?

Completed purchases can be verified on BscScan using the transaction hash shown after the purchase. The sale contract is 0x6e102c2930e3247DABF89807C444a6c813847C61.
